MIRA: Reinforcing Multimodal Reasoning via Deceptive Contextual Augmentation

Zhihan Yin, Jianxin Liang, Yifeng Yao, Nonghai Zhang, Bingyue Peng, Chunguang Qie, Huishuai Zhang, Dongyan Zhao

NeurIPS 2026 · Poster

Reinforcement learning with deceptive visual contexts and group-wise reflection triggers, encouraging multimodal models to revisit perceptual mistakes and recover during reasoning.

Overview
MIRA first generates and filters misleading visual descriptions, then injects them into training contexts. Reflection triggers within rollout groups encourage visual self-correction while mixing in original data to preserve generalization.

FREAK: A Fine-grained Hallucination Evaluation Benchmark for Advanced MLLMs

Zhihan Yin, Jianxin Liang, Yueqian Wang, Yifeng Yao, Huishuai Zhang, Dongyan Zhao

ICLR 2026

A benchmark built from photorealistic images with fine-grained counter-commonsense edits, probing whether advanced multimodal models perceive visual details or fall back on familiar priors.

PDFCodeOpenReviewDataset
Overview
FREAK evaluates fine-grained visual hallucination using edited photorealistic images and a controlled subset for detailed perception analysis. It also investigates the behavior of Chain-of-Thought prompting in this setting.

Veto: A Training-free Framework for Fine-grained Visual Perception via Iterative Negative Filtering

Ruyi Li, Zhihan Yin, Tan Yue, Jianxin Liang, Xian-Feng Han, Huishuai Zhang, Dongyan Zhao

ACM Multimedia 2026

A training-free perception framework that progressively removes distracting image regions, helping vision-language models locate and verify fine-grained visual evidence.

Conference
Overview
Veto uses reasoning-driven negative filtering to narrow the visual field, followed by verifiable visual grounding. The framework operates at inference time and targets detail-sensitive perception in complex, high-resolution scenes.
